Brand

Kodu means home. The mark is a house with a K cut through it and a terracotta window. Use it on ivory, white or ink.

Motion and accessibility

Screens slide in 24 px from the right over 0.28 s. Bars grow from zero over 0.9 s. Everything respects reduced motion. Status is never shown by colour alone, every icon button has a label, touch targets are at least 44 px, and machine translation is always labelled with the original one tap away.
