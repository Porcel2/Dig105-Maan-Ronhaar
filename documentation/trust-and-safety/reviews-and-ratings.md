# Reviews and Ratings

Effective date: 8 November 2026

## 1. Who can leave a review
Only customers who booked and paid through Kodu can review a provider, and only after the job is finished. Each job allows one review, within 30 days. Reviews are written in the customer's language and translated for every reader, with the original available.

## 2. What a review shows
A rating from one to five stars, optional tags such as On time or Tidy work, optional text, the customer's first name and initial, the language they wrote in, and the date.

## 3. Provider replies
Providers can reply publicly to any review once, in their own language. The reply is translated for readers and shown under the review. Replies must follow the [Community Guidelines](./community-guidelines.md): no personal details about the customer and no threats or insults. Providers can edit their reply.

## 4. Private customer ratings
Providers rate customers privately after each job. These ratings are never shown to customers or other providers. Kodu uses them to spot customers who repeatedly cause problems, such as no shows or abusive behaviour, and reviews those accounts.

## 5. How the overall rating is worked out
The rating on a profile is the average of all reviews from the last 24 months. Search ranking also uses reliability (on time arrival and few cancellations), reply time, distance and language match, as described in the Terms of Service.

## 6. Removing reviews
We do not remove a review because it is negative. We remove a review only if it breaks the Community Guidelines, is about a different job or person, contains personal data, or was written in exchange for payment or under pressure. Providers can report a review, and a person decides within 5 working days with a written explanation.
