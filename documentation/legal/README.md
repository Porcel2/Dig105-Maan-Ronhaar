# Kodu Legal Documents

Drafts prepared for the DIG105 prototype. Kodu is a course project and not a registered company, so all names, codes and addresses in square brackets are placeholders. These texts are not legal advice and would need review by a lawyer qualified in Estonian law before real use.

| Document | Covers |
|---|---|
| [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md) | All users: marketplace role, booking, escrow, cancellations, withdrawal right, disputes, translation, ranking, reviews, illegal content reports, liability |
| [Privacy Notice](./privacy-notice.md) | GDPR: data collected, purposes and legal bases, recipients, transfers, retention, rights, supervisory authority |
| [Provider Agreement](./provider-agreement.md) | Providers: independent status, verification, licences, VAT, quotes, fees, payouts, reliability, P2B notice and complaints, DAC7, insurance |
| [Cookie Policy](./cookie-policy.md) | Necessary and consent based cookies and local storage |
| [Legal Notice and Contact](./legal-notice.md) | Operator details, DSA contact point, authorities, payment partner, accessibility |

Laws these drafts are built around: GDPR and the Estonian Personal Data Protection Act; Estonian Law of Obligations Act and Consumer Protection Act (distance contracts, 14 day withdrawal); Digital Services Act (notice and action, statement of reasons, contact point); Platform to Business Regulation (ranking transparency, termination notice, complaints, mediation); DAC7 (platform tax reporting); PSD2 via a licensed payment partner; Estonian Electronic Communications Act (cookies); EU AI Act transparency (machine translation is disclosed); European Accessibility Act.
