# Kodu Trust and Safety

How Kodu keeps both sides safe: who we let on the platform, what we expect from everyone, and what happens when something goes wrong. These policies sit alongside the [legal documents](../legal/) and are written in plain language for users. Like the legal drafts, they are prepared for the DIG105 prototype; names in square brackets are placeholders.

| Policy | Covers |
|---|---|
| [Provider Verification](./provider-verification.md) | Identity, licence, VAT and insurance checks, badges, reverification and removal |
| [Community Guidelines](./community-guidelines.md) | How customers and providers must behave, what is not allowed, and how reports work |
| [Payments, Cancellations and Refunds](./payments-cancellations-refunds.md) | Escrow, materials deposits, change orders, the three cancellation policies, no shows and refunds |
| [Dispute Resolution](./dispute-resolution.md) | Reporting a problem, the timeline, evidence, possible outcomes and appeals |
| [Reviews and Ratings](./reviews-and-ratings.md) | Who can review, provider replies, private customer ratings and removal rules |

## The promises behind these policies
1. Every provider customers can book has had their identity checked.
2. Money is never sent straight to a provider. It is held until the customer confirms the job, or released automatically 72 hours after the visit if nobody reports a problem.
3. A real person reviews every report and every dispute, and explains the decision.
4. Price changes only happen in the app, with the customer's approval.
5. Machine translation is always disclosed, and the original message is always one tap away.
