# Cookie Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Kodu uses a small number of cookies and similar storage in your browser. We ask for your consent before using anything that is not strictly necessary, and you can change your choice at any time in Profile, Privacy and data.

## Strictly necessary
| Name | Purpose | Kept for |
|---|---|---|
| kodu_session | Keeps you logged in | Until you log out, at most 30 days |
| kodu_csrf | Protects forms against cross site attacks | Session |
| kodu_consent | Remembers your cookie choice | 12 months |
| kodu.proto (local storage) | Saves app state such as filters and drafts on your device | Until you log out or clear it |

## Analytics (only with consent)
| Name | Purpose | Kept for |
|---|---|---|
| kodu_an | Counts visits and which screens are used, without identifying you, so we can improve the app | 13 months |

We do not use advertising or cross site tracking cookies.

## Your choice
You can accept or refuse analytics in the cookie banner, change your mind later in the app, or block cookies in your browser. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will stop log in from working.
