# Provider Agreement

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This Agreement is between [Kodu OÜ], registry code [00000000], Tallinn, Estonia (Kodu) and you, the person or company offering services through Kodu (Provider). It applies together with the Terms of Service. If they conflict, this Agreement applies to you as a Provider.

## 1. Your status
You are an independent business. You decide whether to accept a request, what to quote, when you work and how you do the job. Nothing in this Agreement makes you an employee, worker or agent of Kodu. You are responsible for your own taxes, social contributions, insurance and permits.

## 2. Eligibility and verification
To receive bookings you must:

1. be at least 18 and legally allowed to work in Estonia;
2. pass identity verification through our partner;
3. hold any licence or qualification your trade requires in Estonia, including electrical work qualifications where applicable, and keep it valid;
4. register as a sole trader (FIE) or a company, and give us your VAT number if you are VAT registered; companies must provide a VAT number;
5. give us a valid IBAN in your own or your company's name.

You must tell us within 7 days if any of this changes. Kodu may pause your profile until checks are updated.

## 3. Your profile
Your profile must be accurate. Language levels must be honest: Customers rely on them. Prices, areas and availability must be kept current. Photos must be of your own work.

## 4. Quotes and jobs
A quote you send is a binding offer for the described job at the stated price, valid for 24 hours. You may not charge more than the accepted quote unless the Customer accepts a new quote in the app before the extra work. You must arrive at the agreed time, do the work with reasonable skill and care, follow safety rules, and photograph the finished work.

## 5. Fees and payment
Kodu charges the Customer a service fee of 10 percent of labour and materials. You receive the full quoted amount for labour and materials. Money is held by our payment partner until the Customer confirms the job, 72 hours pass after the slot without a dispute, or a dispute is resolved. Payouts are made weekly on Mondays free of charge, or within 30 minutes for a fee of 1 percent if you choose instant payout.

## 6. Cancellations and reliability
If you cancel a paid booking, the Customer is refunded in full and your reliability score falls. Repeated late cancellations or no shows may lead to your profile being paused. If the Customer cancels within 24 hours of the slot, you receive the €15 late cancellation fee.

## 7. Disputes
If a Customer opens a dispute you will be asked for your account and photos within 48 hours. Kodu decides how the held money is released and explains its decision. You keep your legal rights against the Customer.

## 8. Off platform payments
For 12 months after you are first introduced to a Customer through Kodu, you must not arrange to be paid outside Kodu for work with that Customer. Breaking this rule may lead to your account being closed.

## 9. Ranking
Your position in search depends on: languages you speak at B2 or better, availability in the requested slot, distance, rating and reliability. You cannot pay to rank higher.

## 10. Restriction, suspension and termination
Kodu will give you at least 30 days notice, with reasons, before ending this Agreement for convenience. Kodu may restrict or suspend your account earlier, with a statement of reasons, if you break this Agreement, the law or safety rules. You can appeal any restriction through our internal complaint process, which is free and answered within 14 days. You may also use mediation under the EU Platform to Business Regulation; our named mediators are [mediator name and address].

## 11. Tax reporting
Under EU rules (DAC7), Kodu reports to the Estonian Tax and Customs Board the identity and earnings of Providers who earn more than €2,000 or complete 30 or more jobs a year. You will receive a copy of the reported information each January.

## 12. Insurance and liability
You should hold public liability insurance suitable for your trade. You are liable to Customers for damage you cause and for the quality of your work. Kodu's liability towards you is limited to the fees Kodu earned from your jobs in the 12 months before the claim, except where the law does not allow a limit.

## 13. Data
You may use Customer details only to carry out the booked job. After the job you must delete any address or contact details you copied outside the app, unless you need them by law. Each side is a separate controller for the data it holds; see the Privacy Notice.

## 14. Changes
Kodu will give you at least 15 days notice of changes to this Agreement, or longer where the change requires you to make significant adjustments. You may end the Agreement before the change takes effect.

## 15. Law
This Agreement is governed by Estonian law. Courts in Tallinn (Harju County Court) have jurisdiction.
