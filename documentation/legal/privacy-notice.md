# Privacy Notice

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This notice explains how Kodu handles personal data when you use the Kodu website and apps. The controller is [Kodu OÜ], registry code [00000000], [street address], Tallinn, Estonia. You can reach us at [privacy@kodu.ee].

## 1. What we collect
**Account data.** Name, email, phone number, password (stored only as a hash), role (Customer or Provider), app language.

**Profile data.** Languages you speak and your level in each, city and district, home address, housing type, whether a landlord arranges repairs, and your landlord's name and contact details if you give them to us.

**Provider data.** Trade and job types, areas covered, prices, availability, licence or registration number, VAT number, trading form (sole trader or company), IBAN and payout preferences.

**Verification data (Providers).** Photos of an identity document, a selfie and a licence. These are checked by our verification partner and are deleted after the check; we keep only the result and the date.

**Job data.** Requests, descriptions, photos you upload, quotes, bookings, cancellations, disputes and reviews.

**Messages.** Chat messages and their machine translations.

**Payment data.** Payment status, amounts and the last four digits of a card. Full card details go directly to our payment partner and are never stored by Kodu.

**Technical data.** Device type, browser, IP address, log in times and, only if you agree, usage statistics.

## 2. Why we use it and on what legal basis
| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Creating your account, matching Customers with Providers, bookings, chat, translation, payments and payouts | Performance of the contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Verifying Provider identity and licence | Legitimate interest in keeping Customers safe (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Sending the quote to your landlord when you ask us to | Performance of the contract at your request |
| Bookkeeping, tax reporting under DAC7, anti money laundering checks by our payment partner | Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) |
| Preventing fraud, fake reviews and misuse | Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Handling disputes and reports of illegal content | Legitimate interest and legal obligation under the Digital Services Act |
| Tips for settling in Estonia and other marketing emails | Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time |
| Analytics cookies | Consent, see the Cookie Policy |

Data about your languages is used only to match you with Providers. We do not use it to infer nationality or ethnic origin, and we do not use any special category data.

## 3. Who sees your data
**Other users.** Providers see a Customer's first name, languages and district before payment, and the full address only after payment. Customers see a Provider's public profile. A landlord you name sees the job, the quote and the Provider profile, never the chat.

**Service providers acting for us.** Payment processing [Stripe Payments Europe Ltd., Ireland]; identity verification [verification partner]; machine translation [translation provider]; hosting [hosting provider, EU region]; email and SMS delivery [provider]. Each works under a data processing agreement and only on our instructions.

**Authorities.** The Estonian Tax and Customs Board receives annual Provider earnings reports where the DAC7 thresholds are met. We share data with other authorities only where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data.

## 4. Transfers outside the EU
We aim to keep data in the European Economic Area. If a service provider processes data outside the EEA, we rely on an adequacy decision or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, and you can ask us for a copy of the safeguards.

## 5. How long we keep it
| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Account and profile | While your account is open, then deleted within 30 days |
| Identity documents and selfies | Deleted after the verification check |
| Jobs, quotes, payments and invoices | 7 years, as required by the Estonian Accounting Act |
| Chat messages | 2 years after the last message in a conversation, longer only if part of a dispute |
| Reviews | While the Provider's profile exists; shown with your first name and initial only |
| Technical logs | 90 days |

## 6. Your rights
You can ask to access, correct, delete or download your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. Most of this can be done in Profile, Privacy and data. Otherwise write to [privacy@kodu.ee]; we reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon), Tatari 39, 10134 Tallinn, info@aki.ee, or to the authority in your own EU country.

## 7. Automated decisions
Kodu ranks search results automatically, as described in section 9 of the Terms of Service. Ranking does not have legal or similarly significant effects on you. Kodu does not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have such effects; account restrictions are always reviewed by a person.

## 8. Security
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is limited to staff who need it, and payments are handled by a PCI DSS certified partner. If a breach is likely to put your rights at high risk, we will tell you without undue delay.

## 9. Children
Kodu is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect their data.

## 10. Changes
We will tell you in the app before any material change to this notice takes effect.
