# Community Guidelines

Effective date: 8 November 2026

Kodu brings together people from many countries who often do not share a language. These guidelines apply to everyone who uses Kodu: customers, providers and anyone working for a provider.

## 1. Be respectful
1. Treat everyone with courtesy, in chat, in reviews and at the door.
2. No harassment, threats, insults or discrimination based on nationality, language, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or age.
3. Remember that messages are machine translated. If something sounds rude, ask before assuming it was meant that way.

## 2. Be honest
1. Describe the job accurately and add photos when you can.
2. Providers quote honestly and only for work they are qualified to do.
3. Use your real name and your own account. Do not let anyone else work under your profile.
4. Reviews describe your own experience of a real job.

## 3. Keep payments on Kodu
1. All payments go through Kodu, including extra work, which is added with a change order in the app.
2. Asking for or offering cash, bank transfers or other payments outside Kodu is not allowed. It removes the escrow protection for the customer and the payment guarantee for the provider.
3. Sharing phone numbers, emails or payment details in chat before a booking is paid is blocked automatically. Kodu provides a masked phone number once the job is booked.

## 4. Keep each other safe
1. Providers arrive at the booked time or message through Kodu if they are late.
2. Providers respect the customer's home, clean up after the job and take photos of the finished work.
3. Customers make sure the provider can reach the work area safely and tell them about hazards such as pets, asbestos or exposed wiring.
4. If you ever feel unsafe, leave the situation and call 112. Then report it to Kodu.

## 5. Not allowed on Kodu
1. Illegal work, or work that needs a licence the provider does not hold.
2. Fake profiles, fake reviews or paying for reviews.
3. Offensive, sexual or violent content in profiles, photos or chat.
4. Spam, scams and links that try to collect passwords or payment details.
5. Using Kodu to find customers or providers and then moving the work off the platform.

## 6. Reporting
Use Report on any profile, message or review. A person reviews every report, usually within one working day, and we tell you what we decided and why. Reports are confidential: the other person is not told who reported them.

## 7. What happens when someone breaks the rules
Depending on how serious and how repeated it is, we may send a warning, remove content, limit the account, hide a provider from search, or close the account. Every decision comes with a statement of reasons and can be appealed through the help centre within six months.
