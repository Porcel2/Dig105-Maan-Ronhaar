# Payments, Cancellations and Refunds

Effective date: 8 November 2026

## 1. How payment works
1. The customer pays the full total when booking: labour, materials and the Kodu service fee of 10 percent.
2. Payments are processed by our licensed payment partner, [Stripe Payments Europe Ltd.]. Kodu never stores full card numbers.
3. The money is held in escrow. It is released to the provider when the customer confirms the job is done, or automatically 72 hours after the booked visit if no problem has been reported.
4. Providers are paid out every Monday, or the same day with instant payout for a small fee.

## 2. Fixed price services
Providers can publish a menu of standard jobs at a fixed price, such as replacing a tap cartridge. Customers book and pay these straight away without a quote. The fixed price includes labour. If the provider finds that materials or extra work are needed, they use a change order.

## 3. Materials deposits
For jobs with expensive parts, a provider can ask for the cost of materials as a deposit. The quote shows this clearly before the customer pays. The materials amount is paid to the provider when the customer books, so the parts can be bought before the visit. Labour and the rest of the total stay in escrow as usual. If the job is cancelled by the provider, the customer gets the deposit back in full. If the customer cancels after the parts were bought, the provider may keep the deposit and hand over the parts.

## 4. Change orders
If a job turns out bigger than quoted, the provider sends a change order in the app with the extra amount, a reason and a photo. The customer approves or declines it. Approved amounts are added to escrow with the Kodu fee. If the customer declines, the provider finishes the original job at the quoted price, or both sides can agree to cancel with a fair refund. Work done without an approved change order is not paid through Kodu and cannot be charged later.

## 5. Cancellation policies
Each provider chooses one of three policies. It is shown on their profile and on the payment screen before the customer pays.

| Policy | Free cancellation | Late cancellation fee |
|---|---|---|
| Flexible | Until 24 hours before the visit | €15 call out fee |
| Moderate | Until 48 hours before the visit | 25 percent of the job |
| Strict | Until 72 hours before the visit | 50 percent of the job |

The late cancellation fee goes to the provider. Everything else is refunded. A request that has not been paid yet can always be cancelled for free.

## 6. No shows
1. If the provider does not arrive within 30 minutes of the slot without messaging, the customer can cancel for a full refund and the provider's reliability score drops.
2. If nobody lets the provider in and the customer does not reply within 30 minutes, the provider can mark a no show and keeps the late cancellation fee of their policy.

## 7. When a provider cancels
The customer gets a full refund, including any materials deposit, and we help them find another provider. Providers who cancel often lose ranking in search and may be suspended.

## 8. Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually arrive within 5 working days. Partial refunds agreed in a dispute are shown in the job and on the receipt. Customers who are consumers keep their legal rights, including the right of withdrawal described in the Terms of Service.
