# Provider Verification Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

Customers book strangers to come into their homes, so Kodu checks every provider before they can be booked. This policy explains what we check, how long it takes and what the badges on a profile mean.

## 1. What we check

| Check | Who needs it | How we check | Result on the profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity | Every provider | Photo of an Estonian ID card, residence permit card or EU passport, plus a live selfie, checked by our identity partner [ID partner] | Verified ID badge |
| Business details | Companies (OÜ) and sole traders (FIE) | Registry code checked in the Estonian e-Business Register | Company name shown |
| Trade qualification | Trades where Estonian law requires one, such as electrical work and gas work | Professional certificate checked in the Estonian qualifications register (Kutseregister) or the register of economic activities (MTR) | Licensed badge |
| VAT number | Providers registered for VAT | Number checked in the EU VIES service | VAT shown on receipts |
| Liability insurance | Optional, recommended for every trade | Certificate uploaded and checked for cover dates and amount | Insured badge |

Providers can create an account, set up their profile and send quotes while checks are in review. Customers can only book and pay a provider once the identity check has cleared.

## 2. How long it takes
Most identity checks finish within minutes. Licence and insurance checks are done by a person and usually take up to 24 hours on working days. Providers see the status of each check in their profile.

## 3. When a check fails
We tell the provider which check failed and why, for example a blurred photo, an expired document or a certificate that does not match the name on the ID. The provider can upload a new document at any time. After three failed identity attempts the account is reviewed by our team before another attempt is allowed.

## 4. Keeping checks current
1. We store the expiry date of every document and remind the provider 30 days before it runs out.
2. An expired licence removes the Licensed badge and hides the provider from searches for that trade until a new certificate is checked.
3. An expired insurance certificate removes the Insured badge.
4. We may ask any provider to verify their identity again, for example after a change of phone number, bank account or name, or if we see signs that someone else is using the account.

## 5. What badges do not mean
Badges show that a document was valid when we checked it. They are not a guarantee of the quality of the work. Reviews, photos of past work and the reliability score help customers judge that.

## 6. Removal from Kodu
We suspend or remove a provider who gives false documents, lets another person work under their account, asks customers to pay outside Kodu, or seriously breaks the [Community Guidelines](./community-guidelines.md). The provider receives a statement of reasons and can ask us to review the decision, as set out in the Provider Agreement.

## 7. Data we keep
Identity documents are processed by our identity partner and Kodu keeps only the result of the check, the document type and its expiry date. Details are in the [Privacy Notice](../legal/privacy-notice.md).
