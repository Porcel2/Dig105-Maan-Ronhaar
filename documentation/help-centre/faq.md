# Kodu Help Centre: Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: 8 November 2026

## For expats and customers

### Getting started
**What is Kodu?**
Kodu connects people living in Estonia with verified local tradespeople for plumbing, electrical work, painting, cleaning, handyman jobs and moving. You can search by the languages a provider speaks, chat with automatic translation and pay safely through escrow.

**Which cities does Kodu cover?**
Tallinn, Tartu, Narva, Pärnu, Kohtla-Järve, Viljandi, Rakvere, Maardu, Kuressaare and Võru. You can filter by city and then by district.

**Do I need to speak Estonian?**
No. Write in your own language and the provider reads it in theirs. You can also filter for providers who speak your language at B2 level or better.

### Booking
**How do I book a provider?**
Open a profile, tap Request quote, describe the job, add photos and pick a time slot. The provider replies with a written quote. You pay only when you accept it.

**What is a fixed price service?**
Some providers list standard jobs at a fixed price, such as replacing a tap cartridge. You can book and pay these straight away, without waiting for a quote.

**My landlord pays for repairs. Can Kodu handle that?**
Yes. Add your landlord's contact details in your profile. The quote is sent to them for approval before you can pay, and they never see your chat.

**Can I book the same provider again?**
Yes. Tap Book again on a finished job, or save providers with the star on their profile.

### Payment
**When do I pay, and is my money safe?**
You pay when you accept a quote. The money is held in escrow by our payment partner, not sent to the provider. It is released when you confirm the job is done, or automatically 72 hours after the visit if you do not report a problem.

**What does Kodu charge?**
A service fee of 10 percent, shown in every quote before you pay. There are no hidden costs.

**What is a materials deposit?**
For jobs with expensive parts, the provider can ask for the cost of the materials when you book, so they can buy the parts before the visit. The quote shows this clearly. The rest stays in escrow until the job is done.

**The provider says the job is bigger than quoted. What happens?**
They send a change order in the app with the extra amount, a reason and a photo. You decide whether to approve it. Never pay extra in cash.

**How do I pay?**
By card, Apple Pay or Revolut. Kodu never stores your card number.

### Cancelling and problems
**Can I cancel a booking?**
Yes. Each provider has a Flexible, Moderate or Strict cancellation policy, shown on their profile and before you pay. Cancelling before the free cancellation deadline gives a full refund. After it, a late fee goes to the provider.

**Something went wrong with the job. What do I do?**
Open the booking and tap Report a problem before the payment is released. The payment is put on hold, the provider has 48 hours to reply, and if you cannot agree, a Kodu specialist decides within 5 working days.

**When will I get my refund?**
Refunds usually reach your card or account within 5 working days.

### Trust
**How do you check providers?**
Every provider who can be booked has had their identity verified. We also check licences for trades that need one, VAT numbers and, where uploaded, liability insurance. See the Provider Verification policy for details.

**When does the provider see my address?**
They see your district when you send a request, and your full address only after you pay.

**How accurate is the translation?**
Translation is automatic and works well for everyday messages, but it can miss technical terms. The original message is always shown under each translation. If something is unclear, ask, or send a photo.

## For service providers

### Joining
**What do I need to join?**
A phone number, a photo ID, your trade, the areas you cover and a bank account for payouts. Companies also add their registry code and, if registered, their VAT number.

**How long does verification take?**
The identity check usually takes minutes. Licence and insurance checks take up to 24 hours on working days. You can set up your profile and send quotes while you wait. Customers can book you once your ID is checked.

**Does it cost anything to join?**
No. Kodu charges the customer a 10 percent service fee on each job. You receive the full amount of your quote.

### Getting work
**How do customers find me?**
By trade, city, district, language, availability and price. Ranking uses your rating, reliability, reply time, distance and language match. A complete profile ranks higher.

**What is the profile strength score?**
A checklist in your profile: a profile photo, an about text, three photos of your work, an insurance certificate, at least three fixed price services and verified ID. Profiles above 80 percent get up to three times more requests.

**Should I add a service menu?**
Yes, for standard jobs. Customers can book menu items instantly at your fixed price, so you get paid bookings without writing quotes.

**What do Insights show?**
Quote acceptance, reply time, profile views, demand by district, your price against the area average, the busiest times and the most requested jobs, with suggestions to get more work.

### Quotes and jobs
**What kinds of quotes can I send?**
A fixed price, a price range with a maximum, or a paid site visit first. You can save quotes as templates and ask the customer a question before quoting.

**Can I ask for materials up front?**
Yes. Turn on Ask for materials as a deposit in the quote. You receive the materials amount when the customer books.

**What if the job turns out bigger than quoted?**
Tap Job is bigger than quoted and send a change order with the extra amount, a reason and a photo. Wait for the customer's approval in the app before you start the extra work.

**I cannot make the requested time. Do I have to decline?**
No. Suggest another time from your free slots instead.

**Why must I add photos when I finish a job?**
An after photo is required when you mark a job as done. Before and after photos protect you if the customer later reports a problem.

### Getting paid
**When do I get paid?**
When the customer confirms the job, or automatically 72 hours after the visit if they do not report a problem. Payouts go to your bank account every Monday, or the same day with instant payout.

**What happens if a customer cancels late?**
You keep the late cancellation fee of the policy you chose: €15 on Flexible, 25 percent of the job on Moderate, or 50 percent on Strict.

**Do I get receipts for my accounting?**
Yes. Every job has a printable receipt, and you can download a statement of your earnings. Kodu reports annual earnings over €2,000 to the Tax and Customs Board under the DAC7 rules.

### Reviews and safety
**Can I reply to reviews?**
Yes, once per review, publicly and in your own language. Kodu translates your reply.

**What if a customer is difficult?**
Rate customers privately after each job. Only Kodu sees these ratings. You can also report a customer at any time, and if you ever feel unsafe, leave and call 112.

**What if a customer opens a dispute?**
You have 48 hours to reply with your side and photos. You can offer to come back and fix it, offer a partial refund, or explain why the work was done as quoted.

## Still need help?
